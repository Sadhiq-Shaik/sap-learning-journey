# 💡 Key Takeaways

- SAP is built around business processes.
- ERP integrates multiple departments into one system.
- SAP modules work together to complete business operations.
- Every business event generates a business document.
- Business document data is stored in SAP database tables.
- Understanding business is essential before writing ABAP code.
