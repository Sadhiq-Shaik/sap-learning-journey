# Concepts Learned

## ERP (Enterprise Resource Planning)

ERP is software that integrates all business departments such as Sales, Finance, Human Resources (HR), Purchasing, Production, 
and Warehouse into "one centralized system with a single database". This enables real-time data sharing, reduces duplicate work,
improves efficiency, and helps organizations make accurate business decisions.

### Key Features

- One centralized database
- Real-time information sharing
- Integrated business processes
- Better decision making
- Reduced human errors
- Automated workflows

---

## SAP

SAP (Systems, Applications & Products in Data Processing) is one of the world's leading ERP software solutions.

SAP helps organizations manage their entire business through a single integrated system instead of using multiple disconnected software applications.

It allows different departments to work together while sharing the same business data.

### Business Areas Managed by SAP

- Finance
- Sales
- Purchasing
- Warehouse
- Manufacturing
- Human Resources
- Customer Service

---

## Business Process

A Business Process is a sequence of activities performed by different departments to achieve a specific business objective.

Example:

Customer places an order

↓

Sales creates Sales Order

↓

Warehouse checks stock

↓

Finance verifies payment

↓

Shipping delivers product

↓

Invoice generated

↓

Customer receives product

---

## Master Data (MD)

Master Data is relatively stable business information that is created once and reused in multiple business transactions.

Although Master Data can change, it changes infrequently.

### Examples

- Customer
- Vendor
- Material
- Employee
- Company Code
- Plant

---

## Transaction Data (TD)

Transaction Data is the information generated during day-to-day business operations.

It changes continuously as business activities occur.

### Examples

- Sales Order
- Purchase Order
- Invoice
- Goods Receipt
- Payment

# Interview Questions

## Q1. What is ERP?

ERP (Enterprise Resource Planning) is software that integrates all departments of an organization into one centralized system using a single database, 
enabling efficient business operations and real-time information sharing.

---

## Q2. What is SAP?

SAP is one of the world's leading ERP software solutions that helps organizations manage all their business processes through one integrated system.

---

## Q3. What is Master Data?

Master Data is relatively stable information that is created once and reused in multiple business transactions.

Examples include Customer, Vendor, Material, Employee, Company Code, and Plant.

---

## Q4. What is Transaction Data?

Transaction Data is information generated during daily business operations.

Examples include Sales Orders, Purchase Orders, Invoices, Payments, and Goods Receipts.

---

## Q5. What is a Business Process?

A Business Process is a sequence of activities performed by different departments to achieve a business objective.

---

## Q6. Why do companies use SAP instead of Excel?

Companies use SAP because it:

- Integrates all departments
- Eliminates duplicate data
- Provides real-time information
- Reduces human errors
- Automates business processes
- Generates accurate reports
- Supports better business decisions

# Mistakes Identified

## Mistake 1

Initially thought SAP was an Operating System.

### Correction

SAP is ERP Software.

ABAP is the programming language.

Windows/Linux are Operating Systems.

---

## Mistake 2

Initially confused Customer Data is with Transaction Data.

### Correction

Customer Data is Master Data.

Customer Orders are Transaction Data.

---

## Mistake 3

Initially believed Master Data never changes.

### Correction

Master Data changes rarely but can be updated when required.

Example:

Customer changes address.

Vendor updates bank account.

# Key Takeaways

- Business always comes before technology.
- SAP is an ERP software, not a programming language.
- ERP integrates all business departments.
- Master Data is reused across multiple transactions.
- Transaction Data is created every day.
- Understanding business processes is the foundation of SAP development.
