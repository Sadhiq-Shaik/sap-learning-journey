# 💡 Key Learnings from Week 1

- SAP is an integrated ERP system built around business processes.
- Every business process generates business documents.
- Business documents are stored in SAP database tables.
- SAP modules collaborate to complete business operations.
- Organizational Structure defines how companies operate inside SAP.
- ABAP Developers must understand business before writing code.
- Understanding document flow is essential for building accurate SAP reports and applications.
