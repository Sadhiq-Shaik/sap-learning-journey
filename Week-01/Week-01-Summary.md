# 💡 Key Lessons Learned

- SAP is an integrated ERP system designed around business processes.
- Every business process consists of multiple business events.
- Every significant business event creates a business document.
- Business document data is stored in SAP database tables.
- SAP modules work together to complete end-to-end business processes.
- Understanding the business is the first responsibility of an ABAP developer.
